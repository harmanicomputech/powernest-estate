PowerNest ESTATE

Privacy Policy

Effective · Applies to the PowerNest Estate app on Android and the web

Before publishing: fill in the operator name and contact email in the POLICY settings at the bottom of this file.

PowerNest Estate (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by . It helps residential estates manage visitor access, prepaid electricity, estate bills, maintenance and security. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Each estate that uses the app (its management company or residents’ association) decides how the app is used in that estate. For records the estate manages — such as resident lists, bills and gate logs — the estate and the operator are both responsible for protecting your data.

    We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs or SMS messages. We do not collect or store your full card details.

    Device permissions

    • Camera — used only when you choose to scan a visitor’s QR code at the gate (security staff) or to take a photo to attach to a request, report or receipt. Images are not taken in the background.
    • Photos and files — used only when you choose a file to upload, such as a bank receipt or a photo of a fault.
    • Notifications — used to tell you about visitors at the gate, electricity tokens, bills and estate announcements. You can turn categories off in your account settings.

    How we use your information

    • To create and secure your account and confirm you belong to an estate.
    • To issue visitor passes and let security staff verify visitors at the gate.
    • To sell and deliver prepaid electricity tokens to the meter number you enter, and to resolve failed or delayed purchases.
    • To bill estate charges (such as the yearly service charge), take payments, verify offline receipts and show your transaction history and receipts.
    • To handle maintenance requests, incidents, bookings and announcements.
    • To send service emails and notifications, such as tokens, receipts, invitations and password resets.
    • To keep the service safe: preventing fraud and misuse, keeping audit logs and meeting legal and accounting obligations.

    Our legal bases are: performing our agreement with you and your estate, our legitimate interest in running a secure service, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where we ask for it (for example optional notifications).

    We do not sell your personal data, show advertising or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

    Who we share it with

    • Your estate’s management and staff, limited by their role. For example, security officers see visitor passes for their gate, and finance staff see bills and payments.
    • Payment processors (Paystack and Payaza) process online payments. They receive your email address, the amount and a reference. Their own privacy policies apply to the card or bank details you give them.
    • The electricity vending service (Stron) receives the meter number and amount needed to generate a token and to show who the meter is registered to.
    • Service providers who host the app and send email for us, under confidentiality and data-processing terms.
    • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect the safety of residents.

    Some providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA requires.

    How long we keep it

    • Gate visit records: 12 months. QR scan logs: 6 months. Expired visitor passes: 12 months.
    • Electricity purchases, invoices, payments and receipts: as long as needed for accounting, tax and dispute purposes (normally at least 6 years).
    • Account details: while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised after you close it, except records we must keep by law.
    • Security audit logs: kept to investigate misuse and protect residents.

    How we protect it

    • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed; electricity tokens and sessions are encrypted at rest.
    • Access inside each estate is controlled by roles and permissions, and every estate’s data is kept separate.
    • Payments are confirmed directly with the payment processor before any token is issued; important actions are recorded in an audit log.

    No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects you, we will notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

    Your rights

    You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. You can update most details yourself under Account in the app. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

    Deleting your account

    To delete your PowerNest Estate account and its personal data, email from the email address on your account with the subject “Account deletion request”, or ask your estate office. We will confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

    We delete your profile, contact details, saved meters, visitor passes and uploaded files. We keep records the law or the estate’s accounts require — such as invoices, payments, electricity purchase receipts and security audit entries — and remove your name from them where we can.

    Children

    The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Household members under 18 should use the app only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
